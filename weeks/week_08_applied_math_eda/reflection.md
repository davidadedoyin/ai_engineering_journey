# Week 8 reflection

## Prompts

- What is a vector in plain English?
- What does correlation tell us?
- Why are mean and standard deviation useful?
- Why is probability different from certainty?
- What part of this week felt easiest to understand?

## Example answer (example only)

A vector is a list of numbers that can be used together in one calculation. Correlation tells us if two things move together, but it does not prove one caused the other. Mean and standard deviation help us describe a group of values in a compact way. Probability gives us a likelihood, not certainty. The easiest part for me was seeing how NumPy can do math across many values at once.
